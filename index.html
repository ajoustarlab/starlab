Statistical methodology for real-world data

As data grows in volume and complexity, uncovering its underlying patterns becomes essential. STAR Lab studies systematic statistical methods to support decision-making from the large, complex datasets common in medicine, nursing, and environmental science.

Our work spans medical statistics, statistical learning for big data, functional and longitudinal data analysis, and dimension reduction for text — with a consistent emphasis on developing new methodology, not just applying existing tools.
